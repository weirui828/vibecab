Covibe: shaping one piece of work together

Covibe is how a person and an agent shape one piece of work together. You comment; the agent replies; nothing changes until you agree.

How a round works

Read the draft and leave comments where something feels off. The agent answers in the thread, sometimes with a concrete proposal. When you agree, the next Update rewrites only what you agreed to.
